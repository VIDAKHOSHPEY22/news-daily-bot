Needless to say, I was surprised when I was billed $3k USD (plus tax).<p>After contacting Hover's customer support, they told me that there was nothing that they could do about this and they have a no refund policy because "Hover does not have the ability to override or reverse pricing set by the registry. Once a renewal has been processed, we are charged that fee immediately by the registry, and those charges are non-refundable to us."<p>So according to Hover, the .sexy registry (Uniregistry) sets prices as it sees fit, decided to jack up the renewal cost at the last minute because they can, and there's simply nothing they or I can do about it.
